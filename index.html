BACKGROUND

About me

I am a PhD Candidate at The University of Hong Kong, Institute of Data Science, advised by Prof. Xihui Liu and Prof. Yi Ma. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Tsinghua University in 2024, where I worked with Prof. Hang Su and Prof. Jun Zhu.

My research interests span Code for Vision and vision-language code agents, using executable programs to connect visual perception, reasoning, and action. My current work includes code-driven mathematical visual reasoning, video understanding, and multimodal research with tools.

Looking ahead, I want to explore how code can help agents create and edit visual content, from animations to Blender assets and CAD models. My broader interests include unified multimodal models and reinforcement learning, with the goal of making visual reasoning and creation editable and verifiable through code.
